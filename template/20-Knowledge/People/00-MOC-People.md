# People MOC

The people the owner knows and helps: colleagues, classmates, friends, clients. **Check this page
before doing something for someone.** Their note records who they are, what they know, what they
like and how they write, so the work fits them without asking the owner again.

## How this folder works

- **One folder per person:** `People/<first-last>/<first-last>.md`, `type: person`, kebab-case
  ASCII (transliterate accented letters, so `ğ` becomes `g` and `ü` becomes `u`). The identity note
  is the folder note. Notes about the person can sit beside it. Work made *for* them stays with the
  work (a plan folder, a repo) and their note links to it.
- **When the owner introduces someone new, add their folder** and a row in the table above.
- **What a note holds:**
  - who they are to the owner
  - basics: age, school, work
  - what they know
  - what they like: design, tone
  - how they write
  - what has been done for them
  - what is still unknown
- **Only what the owner said or what their own material shows.** Mark a derived fact as derived
  (an age turned into a birth year, say). Ask the owner rather than guess.
- **Private.** Nobody's data goes into a public repo, a public page, or a commit outside the vault.
  Check that the vault's own remote is private before adding anyone.
- **Pronouns:** use the ones the owner uses for that person. If the owner hasn't said, use they/them.
  Never infer pronouns from a name.
