# Knowledge MOC

Reusable knowledge, one concept per note, in `20-Knowledge/<Category>/`. Anything tied to one repo
lives in that repo's `docs/` instead. Add a `## <Category>` section per area as it grows, with one
line per note: `- [[<slug>]] — <one line>`.
